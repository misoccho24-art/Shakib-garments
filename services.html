02 — How an order runs

Four stages, one point of contact.

The same merchandiser stays with your order from enquiry to shipment, so you never have to re-explain the brief.

  1. Stage 01

    Enquiry & sampling

    Send a tech pack or a reference garment. We source the fabric, make a counter sample and agree the fit block with you.

    10–14 daysSample approved
  2. Stage 02

    Costing & order confirmation

    A costed quotation against your quantity and delivery window, then a confirmed order with a booked line slot.

    3–5 daysPrice fixed
  3. Stage 03

    Bulk production

    Cutting, sewing and finishing run as a tracked lot. Cutting, sewing and finishing status is recorded per order, with photographs.

    25–30 daysTracked daily
  4. Stage 04

    Inspection & shipment

    Final AQL audit, packing to your carton specification, documentation prepared and handover to your nominated forwarder.

    5–7 daysDocs complete
