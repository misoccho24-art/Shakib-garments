01 — About us

Who we are.

Sakib Apparels Limited is one of the best knitwear item manufacturers in Bangladesh, working to make satisfied customers of global markets. We manufacture t-shirts, tops, crop tops, hoodies, trousers and more, with the best quality control to ensure the highest comfort. We are also concerned about your fashion, luxury and values.

We are a trusted garment exporting company committed to delivering high-quality apparel to international markets. With a strong focus on quality, responsible production and customer satisfaction, we work closely with buyers and fashion brands to turn their ideas into products that meet global standards.

From sourcing quality fabrics and materials to manufacturing, quality inspection, packaging and shipment, we maintain careful attention to detail throughout every stage of the production process.

Our experienced team understands the importance of timely delivery, consistent quality, competitive pricing and clear communication in building long-term business relationships.

We believe that successful business is built on trust, quality, transparency and commitment. Our goal is not simply to export garments, but to become a reliable long-term sourcing partner for our customers around the world.

03 — Our path

How the factory grew.

2009

First sewing floor

Sakib Apparels opens in Savar with four lines and a single knit t-shirt programme.

2013

First European buyer

A repeat order from a French brand takes the factory into regular export production.

2017

Fleece and bottoms added

Hoodies and knit trousers join the range, doubling monthly capacity.

2021

In-house sampling room

Pattern and sampling brought in-house, cutting development time to under two weeks.

2026

Digital order sheets

Every production lot tracked from cutting to finishing, with photographs against each order.
